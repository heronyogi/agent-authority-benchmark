# Intellectual-property boundary

## Public surface

This repository intentionally publishes only an ontology-neutral behavioral
protocol. Its public vocabulary is limited to concepts required to reproduce
the benchmark: subject, purpose, permission, authority, action, consequence,
observation boundary, contestation, propagation, receipt, and mutation.

## Excluded material

The repository must not contain:

- a private symbolic register or formal ontology;
- canonical morphology names, definitions, or transition matrices;
- private signature names, type schemes, ports, or effect rows;
- composition, routing, closure, or validation laws imported from a private
  system;
- mappings from benchmark cases to private formal coordinates;
- private correspondence or identifiable personal examples; or
- claims that the public harness reconstructs or validates a private ontology.

## Implementation independence

Any conforming implementation may satisfy the protocol. A public case states
observable conditions and consequences, not the internal method that must be
used to produce them.

## Provenance

Public artifacts should record their contributors and source history. Private
research materials and public benchmark contributions should remain traceable
as distinct bodies of work.
